## Timing classification

**This is a PROSPECTIVE capture set, not retrospective.**

The validation request described the game as already begun or completed and asked
for `--capture-mode retrospective-development`. Two facts made that impossible to
follow literally, and both are recorded here rather than worked around:

1. **The game had not started.** At capture time the MLB schedule feed reported
   gamePk 823194 as `Pre-Game` / `Preview`, first pitch
   `2026-07-26T20:05:00+00:00`. The nine captures ran between `18:53:19Z` and
   `18:54:56Z` — roughly 70 minutes before first pitch.
2. **`retrospective-development` is not a capture mode.** The operator CLI accepts
   `prospective` or `retrospective` only, and application code was not to change.

Labelling a genuinely pre-first-pitch capture as retrospective would have written
a false timing claim into nine immutable, digest-pinned bundles, in the one
dimension this pipeline treats as its central evidential property. The Product
Owner was asked and chose **capture now as prospective**. Each bundle therefore
records `classification: prospective` and `captured before scheduled start:
true`, proven from recorded manifest instants and re-proven on every replay.
